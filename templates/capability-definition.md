# Capability Definition Template

## Name

A concise label for the enduring business ability.

## Definition

One or two sentences explaining what the organisation must be able to do.

## Business outcome / purpose

Why the capability exists and which business outcome or responsibility it supports.

## Scope included

What belongs inside the capability boundary.

## Scope excluded

What is deliberately outside the capability boundary.

## Parent capability

The broader capability, if the model is hierarchical.

## Child capabilities

More specific enduring abilities contained within this capability, if needed.

## Key information concepts

Important business information used or produced by the capability. Do not turn this into a data model.

## Relevant measures

A small number of outcomes or indicators that could help assess whether the capability is effective.

## Notes / uncertainty

Material ambiguity, missing evidence or modelling questions.

---

## Keep separate

Do not make these part of capability identity:

- current owner or team;
- application/platform;
- vendor;
- location;
- process/workflow;
- project/programme;
- current maturity score.

They can be mapped later as separate views.
