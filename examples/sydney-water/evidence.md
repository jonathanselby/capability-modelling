# Evidence Basis — Sydney Water

## Observed from official public material

Sydney Water describes itself as Australia's largest water utility providing:

- drinking water;
- wastewater;
- recycled water;
- some stormwater services;

to Greater Sydney, including Sydney, the Blue Mountains and the Illawarra.

Its public material also demonstrates enduring responsibilities around:

- water quality and monitoring;
- customer service and billing/pricing interaction;
- maintaining and investing in major infrastructure;
- water conservation/demand reduction;
- environmental performance and healthy waterways;
- recycled-water supply;
- wastewater treatment/recovery;
- regulatory/statutory reporting;
- supplier/procurement activity;
- safety and workforce operation.

The 2024–25 annual-report page notes engagement with customers, government and regulators in relation to pricing, ongoing water-efficiency programmes and corporate safety performance.

## Implementation evidence kept separate

Publicly named physical or change artefacts include treatment/recycling facilities, mains, reservoirs, pumping stations and major projects. These are not automatically capability names.

Likewise, `Future Ready` is described as a programme/change context and is not treated as an enduring capability.

## Unknown from public evidence

The public evidence does not establish:

- internal capability ownership;
- detailed operations-centre structure;
- full asset-management decomposition;
- exact application/data architecture;
- internal maturity;
- complete treatment-process taxonomy;
- detailed emergency-management accountabilities.

Those gaps remain explicit.
