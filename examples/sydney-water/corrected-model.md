# Corrected Candidate Capability Model — Sydney Water

This is an architectural inference from the dated public source set, not an official Sydney Water taxonomy.

```text
Customer Management
Water Supply Management
Wastewater Management

Stormwater Management

```

## Selected Level 2 decompositions

```text
Water Supply Management
├── Supply Planning
├── Water Production Management
├── Water Distribution Management
└── Supply Continuity Management

Network & Asset Management
├── Asset Planning
├── Asset Maintenance Management
├── Asset Renewal Management
└── Asset Performance Management

Water Quality Management
├── Quality Standard Management
├── Quality Monitoring
├── Quality Incident Management
└── Quality Assurance & Reporting
```

## Why these are at the chosen level

Treatment plants, reservoirs, mains and pumping stations are assets/facilities mapped to capabilities rather than capability identities.

`Water Quality Testing` is part of the broader enduring ability to govern water quality, while `Customer Billing` sits within Revenue Management rather than occupying the same level as enterprise-scale service abilities.

Projects and named programmes map to the capabilities they change.
