# Sydney Water — Public-Source Worked Example

This example applies the capability-modelling method to public Sydney Water material.

It is **not** Sydney Water's official capability model.

## Modelling question

Can a regulated infrastructure utility be modelled at the same architectural granularity as the logistics example without turning assets, treatment plants, projects or operational processes into capabilities?

## Source basis

Official Sydney Water sources retrieved 15 October 2026:

- About us: https://www.sydneywater.com.au/about-us.html
- Annual report page / Annual Report 2024–25: https://www.sydneywater.com.au/about-us/our-publications/annual-report.html
- Reports and publications: https://www.sydneywater.com.au/about-us/our-publications/reports-publications.html

The public material describes Sydney Water as Australia's largest water utility and identifies drinking water, wastewater, recycled water and some stormwater services for Greater Sydney, together with statutory, environmental, customer and infrastructure responsibilities.
