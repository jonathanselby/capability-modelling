# Example Capability Definitions

Selected definitions from the fictional Northstar model.

## Equipment Availability Management

**Definition**  
The ability to determine, reserve and communicate whether suitable equipment is available to meet a customer requirement at the required time and location.

**Business outcome / purpose**  
Enable reliable rental commitments while balancing fleet utilisation and operational constraints.

**Scope included**
- availability determination;
- reservation of fleet capacity;
- visibility of committed versus available equipment;
- communication of availability constraints.

**Scope excluded**
- acquiring new fleet;
- maintaining equipment;
- physically delivering equipment;
- negotiating the rental agreement.

**Key information concepts**  
Equipment, fleet item, reservation, location, serviceability status, rental period.

**Relevant measures**  
Commitment accuracy, reservation conflicts, unavailable-demand rate.

---

## Maintenance Management

**Definition**  
The ability to plan, perform and govern maintenance so rental equipment remains safe, serviceable and economically useful.

**Business outcome / purpose**  
Maintain fleet reliability and availability while meeting safety and operating obligations.

**Scope included**
- planned and corrective maintenance;
- serviceability assessment;
- maintenance scheduling;
- maintenance completion records;
- maintenance parts coordination.

**Scope excluded**
- fleet acquisition and disposal decisions;
- customer rental contracting;
- supplier relationship governance beyond maintenance-specific consumption.

**Key information concepts**  
Fleet item, maintenance requirement, work order, serviceability status, part, maintenance history.

**Relevant measures**  
Fleet downtime, maintenance compliance, repeat faults, maintenance cost.

---

## Rental Agreement Management

**Definition**  
The ability to establish, amend and close agreements governing a customer's temporary use of equipment.

**Business outcome / purpose**  
Create clear and enforceable rental commitments covering equipment, duration, price and customer obligations.

**Scope included**
- quotation-to-agreement transition;
- rental terms and duration;
- extensions and amendments;
- agreement closure.

**Scope excluded**
- general customer relationship management;
- equipment availability calculation;
- physical fulfilment;
- revenue collection.

**Key information concepts**  
Customer, rental agreement, equipment class/item, price, rental period, terms.

**Relevant measures**  
Agreement accuracy, amendment rate, disputed terms, closure timeliness.

## Why definitions matter

Names alone invite different interpretations. The definition and explicit scope make overlaps visible and give reviewers something substantive to challenge.
