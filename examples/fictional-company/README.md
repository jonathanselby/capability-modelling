# Northstar Equipment Hire

This is a fictional company created only to demonstrate the capability-modelling method.

Northstar hires construction and landscaping equipment to commercial and household customers. It owns and maintains a fleet, operates several branches, accepts online and branch bookings, delivers some equipment to customer sites, buys parts and services from suppliers, invoices commercial customers and takes immediate payment from others.

The example deliberately starts with a poor but plausible first draft assembled from visible organisational, technology and process language.

Then it rebuilds the model around enduring business abilities.
