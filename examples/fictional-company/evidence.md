# Evidence Basis — Northstar Equipment Hire

Northstar Equipment Hire is fictional. The evidence set is therefore the scenario stated in the example rather than external research.

## Supplied evidence

Northstar:

- hires construction and landscaping equipment to commercial and household customers;
- owns and maintains a fleet;
- operates several branches;
- accepts online and branch bookings;
- delivers some equipment to customer sites;
- buys parts and services from suppliers;
- invoices commercial customers;
- takes immediate payment from other customers.

## Directly supported business responsibilities

The scenario supports enduring needs around:

- customers and customer relationships;
- fleet ownership and lifecycle;
- understanding equipment availability;
- rental agreements;
- fulfilment of rental commitments;
- equipment maintenance and serviceability;
- revenue collection/accounting;
- supplier relationships;
- workforce and enterprise administration.

## Implementation-shaped evidence deliberately kept separate

The worked first draft introduces plausible labels such as branch/location names, portals, teams, workshops, software products and transformation programmes to demonstrate anti-pattern detection. These are teaching artefacts, not additional business facts.

## Unknowns

The scenario does not establish:

- detailed regulatory obligations;
- insurance/claims operating model;
- fleet financing structure;
- exact pricing model;
- whether transport is owned or outsourced;
- detailed procurement categories;
- organisational structure.

The corrected model does not invent detailed capabilities to fill those gaps.
